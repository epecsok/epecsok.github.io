I am a third-year PhD student in the <a href = "https://www.ling.upenn.edu/">Department of Linguistics</a> at the University of Pennsylvania. I am affiliated with the Penn Meaning Lab and I am co-advised by <a href = "https://www.florianschwarz.net/">Florian Schwarz</a> and <a href = "https://web.sas.upenn.edu/paloma-jeretic/">Paloma Jeretič</a>. I am also pursuing a Dual Master of Arts in Statistics and Data Science.

Before coming to Penn, I received a BA in Linguistics and Applied Mathematics from Cornell University, where I was advised by Helena Aparicio and Kathryn Mann. I am affiliated with the <a href= "https://lime-lab-cornell.github.io/">Linguistic Meaning Lab</a> at Cornell.
